## What I work on

I'm a senior technology consultant at Ernst & Young. Since 2022 I've been a senior Android engineer on Chick-fil-A CommercePoint, the kitchen display application aimed at 3,200+ restaurants and more than 45,000 devices. A lot of the job is observability, telemetry, and performance for that app, alongside the Android running on the devices themselves.

Some of what that turned into:

- Telemetry cost down by about a quarter, by filtering, sampling, and removing what we didn't need
- Android client log coverage up to about 80%, with attribution notes so an incident is less of a treasure hunt
- Monitors and SLIs for the critical paths, and a way to keep them current as the product changes
- About a dozen production hotfixes, and an HTTP fallback so a database failure can degrade gracefully
- LaunchDarkly release workflows, recurring release-candidate builds, and Jira templates that a few other product teams picked up

In 2021 I was a front-end developer for an oil and gas client, building React and TypeScript storefront pieces against their APIs.

Most days the stack is Kotlin, Java, Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, React, and Node, with HTML, CSS, and C++ in the mix. Operations lean on Android Studio, Datadog, OpenTelemetry, GitHub, CI, LaunchDarkly, Jira, and ServiceNow.
