```text
Problem: TODO_PROBLEM_TITLE (TODO_TRACK)
Outcome: solved | partial | stuck
Gap: TODO_MISTAKE_OR_MISSING_IDEA
Next: TODO_TOPIC_TO_DRILL
```

Time box: 25 minutes for code, 20 minutes for a design prompt, unless the project TODO says otherwise.
